# Gleam release notes, September 2026

## TL;DR
Gleam 3.0 is the current release, shipped on 2026-09-02, and it replaces the 2.1 line. It ships 15 exporters.

## What changed
Gleam 3.0 is the current release. The 2.1 line no longer receives fixes. Version 3.0 ships 15 exporters and records token counts on every span.

## Upgrading
Projects on 2.1 can upgrade without changing their trace format.
