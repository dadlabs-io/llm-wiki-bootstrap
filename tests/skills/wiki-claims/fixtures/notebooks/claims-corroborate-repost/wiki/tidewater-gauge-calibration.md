# Tidewater gauge calibration notes

## TL;DR
The Tidewater pressure gauge needs recalibrating every 90 days, and the procedure takes about 20 minutes.

## Procedure
Zero the gauge at atmospheric pressure, then apply the 10 bar reference and adjust the span screw until the reading matches. The author logs each calibration in a shared sheet.
