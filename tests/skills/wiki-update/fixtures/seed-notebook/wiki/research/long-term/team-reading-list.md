# Reading list for new team members

## TL;DR

Three general books that new team members borrow from the office shelf in their first month, with a line on why each is on the list.

## The list

- A short book on writing clearly for colleagues who are in a hurry.
- A history of how a famous bridge was planned and rebuilt.
- A field guide to the meeting habits of small teams.
