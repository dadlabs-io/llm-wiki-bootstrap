# Conference travel checklist

## TL;DR

A short packing and booking list for a team member travelling to a conference. Not yet checked against the venue's own instructions.

## Before leaving

- Book the train and the hotel two weeks ahead.
- Print the badge confirmation.
- Pack a charger, a spare cable and a water bottle.
